Teacher's Corner

How to use this page

1. Read “Meet Shawn” aloud; ask the student to find the three colors. 2. In “Work”, turn the cards and make new sentences (She works as… / She works at…). 3. Read the timeline and ask the student to say the base form of each blue verb. 4. Use the Feelings cards for speaking: “How do you feel before a test?” 5. Finish with the questions, the quiz and “My Turn”.

Extra ideas

Mime a feeling and let the student guess. Ask “Where do you work/study?” and “What does your mother work as?” Ask about yesterday: “How did you feel?” Practise the pronunciation of -ed endings: played /d/, started /id/, stopped /t/.
